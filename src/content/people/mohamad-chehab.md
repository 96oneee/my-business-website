Mohamad completed his bachelor's degree in Biology with a minor in Chemistry at AUB and is now a second-year master's student in the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Genetics, AUB Faculty of Medicine. He is currently conducting translational research on inborn errors of metabolism, integrating clinical genomics and biochemical investigation.

His interest lies at the interface between the chemical and biological sciences, using chemistry as a framework to explain biological phenomena, whether in courses or in the lab. He aims to build on this foundation and pursue an MD degree, bridging the gap between basic and clinical sciences.
