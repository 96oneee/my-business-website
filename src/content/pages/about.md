sections:
  - heading: Purpose & Perspective
    text: >-
      We are curious about one big question: what do genetic changes actually do? Our lab moves
      beyond identifying genes and variants to experimentally uncovering their biological
      consequences. From cancer-driving alterations to rare genetic variants, we use functional
      genomics to turn genomic observations into biological understanding.
  - heading: How It Started
    text: >-
      The lab was established at the American University of Beirut in 2025, bringing together years
      of experience in cancer genetics, CRISPR screening, and in vivo disease modeling. It started
      with a simple idea: build a research program where genomics meets experimentation, while
      creating opportunities for young scientists to ask ambitious questions and learn by doing.
  - heading: Lab Overview
    text: >-
      We are a growing and collaborative group working at the intersection of genetics, cancer
      biology, and biotechnology. We combine CRISPR/Cas9 genome editing, molecular and cellular
      biology, transcriptomics, and in vivo models to study cancer and rare genetic diseases. We
      especially enjoy projects where the answer is not obvious, and where the right experiment can
      turn a genomic clue into a biological story.
