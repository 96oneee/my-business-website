Our lab investigates the earliest molecular and cellular events that push normal lung cells toward malignancy. We study how oncogenic mutations, loss of tumor suppressors, environmental carcinogens, and changes in the immune microenvironment cooperate during this process.

By modeling these early stages experimentally and following how they evolve over time, we hope to identify the changes that distinguish a cell that remains normal from one that eventually becomes cancer, opening opportunities for earlier detection, prevention, and intervention.
