We use cutting-edge CRISPR/Cas9 technologies, multiplexed genetic approaches, and in vivo cancer models to uncover genes that control tumor initiation, growth, and progression. Rather than studying genes one at a time, we are particularly interested in understanding how genetic alterations interact and how their effects change within the complex environment of a living tumor.

By combining functional screens with molecular and genomic analyses, we aim to reveal unexpected vulnerabilities and biological relationships that could ultimately point toward new therapeutic strategies.
